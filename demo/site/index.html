Published by its own machine

This page is served by a laptop. The laptop put its name in the public DNS itself, with its own key: no account, no registrar's web form.

  • demo.srp.dev.zenr.io, with this site as a browsable service (RFC 9665, DNS-SD Service Registration Protocol);
  • demo.dev.zenr.io, a name held by its key (RFC 9664, leases on DNS updates), whose records point here over IPFS too.

When the laptop goes away, so do its records; its keys keep the names for it.
